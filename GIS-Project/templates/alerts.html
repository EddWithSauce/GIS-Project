Alerts & Safety Info

What each status means + recommended actions

Normal
LOW
  • Water level is within safe range.
  • Rainfall is none to light.
  • Continue monitoring.
Recommended actions: Monitor
Warning
MEDIUM
  • Water level rising; possible overflow in some areas.
  • Moderate rainfall or upstream surge may occur.
  • Prepare emergency kit; coordinate with officials.
Recommended actions: Prepare / Monitor
Critical
HIGH
  • High water level; flooding likely/ongoing.
  • Heavy rainfall or rapid rise detected.
  • Follow evacuation orders immediately.
Recommended actions: Evacuate
Notes for Integration (Later)

  • Store readings in a database, then use /api/history from real data.
  • Enable ML by loading your trained model and updating /api/ml/predict.
